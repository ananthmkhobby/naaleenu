# Lunchbox Decision Research

## Research summary

Lunchbox is a strong adjacent problem to breakfast because the core pain is still decision load. For working homemakers and parents, the hard part is often deciding what can be packed quickly, travels well, suits family preferences and avoids repetition.

## Findings

Working parents often reduce meal effort by simplifying, speeding up, planning ahead and relying on practical repeatable patterns. Lunchbox decisions add extra constraints compared with breakfast: food should travel well, stay safe, be acceptable to the eater after several hours and sometimes include a small side such as curd, fruit or snack.

Lunchbox-specific research points to information overload and pressure around balancing nutrition, child preference, school or workplace expectations and what can actually be packed in the morning.

Food safety should influence future recommendations. Perishable packed lunches need appropriate temperature control and insulated lunch bags or cold sources when relevant. Hot foods need a thermos-style path if they are expected to stay hot.

## Product decision

Lunchbox should be added as a meal mode, not a separate planner dashboard. The product promise remains: one practical recommendation first, full list only when requested.

The app now supports:

- Breakfast and Lunchbox meal switch on the decision screen.
- Separate lunchbox catalog.
- Lunchbox-specific Breakfast Book filtering.
- Favorites shared across meal modes but applied by dish ID.
- Custom family dishes saved with the active meal type.
- Custom family lunchbox dishes can be sent into the recommendation engine from local storage.

## Future lunchbox extensions

- Add side pairing rules such as curd, fruit, sundal or pickle.
- Add “packs well cold,” “needs thermos,” “messy for kids,” and “office reheating available” metadata.
- Add previous-night prep and leftover reuse as stronger lunchbox scoring factors.
- Add weekly repetition guardrails, but avoid turning the core screen into a complex calendar.
